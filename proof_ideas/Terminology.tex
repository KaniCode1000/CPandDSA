	\section{Definitions}
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item \textbf{Theorms:} Most important technical statements are labelled as such.
		\item \textbf{Lemma:} A technical statement that assists with the proof of a theorem.
		\item \textbf{Corollary:} A statement that follows immediately from an already-proven result.
		\item \textbf{Proposition:} A stand-alone technical statement that isn't particularly important in their own right.
 	\end{enumerate}
